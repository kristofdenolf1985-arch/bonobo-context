## Question

What should members have done by the time raids open, the week after
1 Dec 2026?

## Decision

- Pick one main early, and be willing to play what the roster needs.
- Tell the officers your main's class and role by [date].
- Be raid-ready when raids open: max level, pre-raid gear from dungeons and
  quests, attunements and resistance gear as the content needs.
- Pick a profession that supplies the raid; officers coordinate the spread.
- Build up gold and materials for consumables ahead of time.

## Why

Proposed by Brightmir on 29 Oct 2026 for Fun Police and adapted to Bonobo.
Officers need the class and role spread before the first raid to build a
roster, and leadership has said professions that supply the raid will matter.

`expectations.md` gets a "Before raids open" section.
