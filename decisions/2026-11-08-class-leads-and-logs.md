## Question

Who sets the standard for each class, and how do raiders improve between
raids?

## Decision

- Each class has a class lead who sets the rotation and gear baseline for
  their class and publishes the required consumables per role and raid.
- Raids are logged on Warcraft Logs. Raiders review their own logs; what
  counts is improvement after feedback.

## Why

Proposed by Brightmir on 29 Oct 2026 for Fun Police and adapted to Bonobo. It
gives rules 4 and 6 (be prepared, keep improving) something concrete to
measure against. A Bonobo guild on Warcraft Logs is already planned.

## What changes

`expectations.md` gets a "Getting better" section; the glossary entry for
class lead no longer says open.
