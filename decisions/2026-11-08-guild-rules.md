## Question

Which rules does Bonobo hold its members and raiders to?

## Decision

The ten rules posted in #rules-and-expectations: respect, criticism and
improvement, play for the team, be prepared and reliable (90% attendance),
raid comms, ask learn and contribute, take accountability, no politics,
serious misconduct, officer escalation.

## Why

Most members came from Fun Police and already knew these rules; they match
the Fun Police rules of 24 Sep 2026.

## What changes

`expectations.md` lists the ten rules as **rule**.
