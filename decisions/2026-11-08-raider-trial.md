## Question

How does a new player earn a raider spot?

## Decision

A trial of 3 weeks or 6 raids, whichever comes later. The trial passes with
at least 90% attendance, prepared for every raid, no repeated avoidable deaths,
and acting on feedback. A class lead and one officer review it. The outcome is
Raider, a longer trial, or Social. During the trial, loot goes to raiders
first; officers have the final say.

## Why

Proposed by Brightmir on 29 Oct 2026 for Fun Police and adapted to Bonobo. It
gives applicants a clear bar and puts the call with people who saw them raid.
