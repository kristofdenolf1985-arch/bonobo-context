What we expect

The ten rules

From #rules-and-expectations on the Discord. Read the full text there; this is the short version.

  1. Respect

    Banter is fine. Toxicity, ego, loot drama and feuds are not.

  2. Criticism and improvement

    Give feedback constructively and take it well. Nobody is above scrutiny.

  3. Play for the team

    Mechanics and the group come before personal gain.

  4. Be prepared and reliable

    Gear, consumables, raid items, add-ons, WeakAuras and keybinds sorted. 90% attendance, on time, sober and rested.

  5. Raid comms

    Keep comms clear. Banter can wait until after the pull.

  6. Ask, learn and contribute

    Learn your class and the encounters, share what you know, keep improving.

  7. Take accountability

    Own your mistakes and learn from them. No hiding or blaming.

  8. No politics

    Keep politics out of guild chat and voice.

  9. Serious misconduct

    Racism, bigotry, harassment, doxxing, threats or sharing personal information can mean immediate removal.

  10. Officer escalation

    Sort minor issues privately first, then bring them to an officer.
