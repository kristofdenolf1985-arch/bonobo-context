# Bonobo

<Bonobo> is a Horde raiding guild on WoW Forever, on the EU PvP realms. The
guild's Discord server is called Bonobo (it used to be Peter Pan Syndrome).

## People

| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| Shoei | Guild leader. Sets the rules and takes the decisions |
| Brightmir | Officer. Owns applications, recruitment, the guild's AI work and this repo |
| Officers | Review and merge changes to this repo |

Add other officers and class leads here as they are named.

## Who we are

- Serious about raiding, social in tone (**rule**).
- A serious raiding guild with a hardcore mindset. Not a speedrun guild: speed
  is not the goal (**direction**, Shoei, 6 Nov 2026).
- Forever has no world buff meta, so being prepared means consumables, gear,
  add-ons and knowing the fights.

## Direction

Where leadership says the guild is heading. None of it is policy yet.

- Standards start now and tighten once people reach 60 and the meta settles
  (Shoei, 5 and 6 Nov 2026).
- Nobody is told which race to play before 60 (Shoei, 5 and 6 Nov 2026).
- Professions that supply the raid will matter for raid spots. Expect to be
  asked to change profession if the raid needs it (Shoei, 6 Nov 2026).
- Players who fall behind on performance improve in the alt raid, or step back
  from raiding (Shoei, 6 Nov 2026).
- A Bonobo guild on Warcraft Logs, linked to the Discord, is planned (Shoei,
  8 Nov 2026).
- Applications will move to a recruitment section on the Discord with OXM, the
  guild manager. Until then Brightmir handles them (8 Nov 2026).

## History

Many Bonobo members raided with <Fun Police>. On Classic Era (2021 to 2023)
Fun Police was a speedrun guild that cleared every raid every week
(**practice**). Its Forever guild folded on 5 Nov 2026, when its leader
Harkie left. Bonobo's ten rules match the rules Fun Police posted on
24 Sep 2026. Fun Police's speed focus did not carry over.
