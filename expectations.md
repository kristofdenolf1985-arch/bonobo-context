# What the guild expects

From the ten rules in #rules-and-expectations on the Bonobo Discord (Shoei,
8 Nov 2026). This page is a summary: re-read the Discord before quoting a rule
word for word. Everything here is **rule** unless marked otherwise.

## The ten rules

1. **Respect.** Banter is welcome. Toxicity, ego, loot drama, feuds and
   harassment are out.
2. **Criticism and improvement.** Give feedback constructively and take it
   well. Nobody is above scrutiny.
3. **Play for the team.** Mechanics and the group come before personal gain
   and personal numbers.
4. **Be prepared and reliable.** Gear, consumables, raid items, add-ons,
   WeakAuras and keybinds sorted before the raid. 90% attendance, on time,
   sober and rested.
5. **Raid comms.** A working mic, quiet during calls, banter after the kill.
6. **Ask, learn and contribute.** Know your class and the encounters, ask
   questions before the raid, share what you know, keep improving.
7. **Take accountability.** Own your mistakes and learn from them.
8. **No politics** in guild chat or voice.
9. **Serious misconduct** (racism, bigotry, harassment, doxxing, threats,
   sharing personal information) can mean immediate removal.
10. **Officer escalation.** Sort minor issues privately first, then bring
    them to an officer. Steps: discussion, warning, further action, removal.

## Every raid

- 90% attendance. Report an absence as early as possible so a replacement can
  be arranged.
- Prepared: see rule 4.
- Know the encounter before the pull.

Raid days, number of raids, loot and consumable standards are **open**: see
`decisions/`.

## Ranks

**Direction** (Shoei, 8 Nov 2026):

- Until people reach 60, every active player is Social.
- Raider roles and raider-only channels come after 60.
- Players who aren't active keep a basic role with public access only.
